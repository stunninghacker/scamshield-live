Six rules that stop most scams

  1. Never share an OTP, PIN, CVV or password, with anyone, for any reason.
  2. Police and CBI never arrest anyone on a video call, and never ask you to transfer money.
  3. Don't allow camera, location or notifications on a site you didn't plan to use them on.
  4. Read the link before you tap it. Short links and odd endings like .xyz are a warning.
  5. You never pay or enter a PIN to receive money. A PIN only ever sends it.
  6. Not sure? Stop and report. Call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.
